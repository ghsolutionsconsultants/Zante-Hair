Our story

Born from a name. Built around her.

Zante Beauty isn’t about giving you a different identity through hair. It is about styling freedom, while you embrace and complement the hair you already have.

Zama+Asante=Zante

Zama + Asante

Zante Beauty began in 2021 and launched nationwide in 2026, inspired by the bond between a mother and daughter. The name Zante joins two names, Zama and Asante, to create something of our own.

We believe beautiful hair should complement who you are.

We focus on carefully selected hair that works with your natural texture, makes everyday styling easier and gives you the freedom to switch up your look, without complicated installs or unnecessary tension.

From quick everyday styling to a more polished look for special occasions, our pieces are designed to give you options.

We are drawn to kinky straight because it is a beautiful middle ground: the fullness and character of natural hair, with the versatility of straight styling.

At Zante Beauty, we celebrate the journey of healthy hair, the freedom to experiment and the beauty of wearing your hair your way.
