Returns, refunds and exchanges

We want you to love your hair

Buying hair online takes trust, so we keep our returns process clear.

Half Wigs

All original packaging, attachments, bands and tamper-evident hygiene tags must be intact. The unit must not have been worn, altered or styled.

U-Part Wigs

The U-part opening, bands and attachments must not be cut or altered, and the unit must be in its original packaging.

How to request a return

  1. Contact us with your order confirmation details.
  2. Give your order number and the reason for the return.
  3. Wait for our return instructions before sending anything back.
  4. Pack the unit securely in its original packaging.
  5. Once we receive and inspect it, we process the refund that applies.
