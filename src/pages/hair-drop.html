Coming up:

    How long will I wait?

    Allow about 3 to 4 weeks from your drop's cut-off for production, international shipping and our Ready Ritual. Local courier time comes after dispatch.

    International shipping and clearance can vary, so delays are sometimes possible. We keep you updated at every step.

    Delivery

    • PostNet to PostNet: R109, about 2 to 3 working days to main centres.
    • Aramex store-to-door: price shown at checkout, about 24 to 72 hours to main centres.

    Remote and regional areas can take longer.

    You will always know where your hair is

    Shipping and delivery

    From our hands to yours

    We want your Zante experience to be just as exciting when your order arrives as when you placed it.

    When will my order be dispatched?

    Allow about 3 to 4 weeks from your drop's cut-off for your unit to be ready for local dispatch. Once it has arrived, passed inspection and finished our Ready Ritual, we pack it for the next Monday dispatch.

    Tracking your order

    Once your parcel is dispatched, we send your tracking details to the contact details you gave us. Please check your delivery details and phone number when you order.

    Important delivery information

    • Local courier times start after dispatch, not from the date you paid for your preorder.
    • Courier times are estimates and can change because of location, public holidays or courier delays.
    • Please give accurate delivery details and collect promptly where collection is needed.
    • We will tell you about any extra courier charges for wrong addresses, failed deliveries or re-delivery.
