The Zante Hair Guide

Half Wig or U-Part?

Not sure which style is right for you? Here is a quick guide.

Choose a Half Wig if

  • You enjoy switching looks: push back, flip over, ponytail, half up half down and more.
  • You want to include your own hair in your style.
  • You like wearing a headband or playing with leave-out.
  • You want a quick grab-and-go wig.
  • You want a removable alternative to a traditional sew-in.
Shop the Half Wig

Choose a U-Part if

  • You love a seamless, natural-looking style with no lace frontal.
  • You like a sew-in look without the restrictions.
  • You are comfortable leaving out a section of your own hair.
  • You prefer a middle or side-parted look.
  • You want a removable wig without a traditional installation.
Shop the U-Part

The Zante Hair Drop

Good hair is worth planning for.

Two scheduled drops every month, so you can secure your style and length instead of waiting for random restocks.

  1. Choose your lookPick the Half Wig or U-Part Wig, choose your length and pay in full at checkout.
  2. Secure your orderYour order joins the next available drop.
  3. Production beginsWhen the drop closes on Monday, your order goes to our factory.
  4. Your hair travels to usAllow about two weeks for international shipping after production.
  5. The Zante finishWe receive, inspect and pre-wash every unit, then pack it for the next Monday dispatch.
  6. Delivery to youWe dispatch on Mondays and send your tracking details.

Our story

Born from a name. Built around her.

Zante Beauty began in 2021 and launched nationwide in 2026, inspired by the bond between a mother and daughter. The name Zante joins two names, Zama and Asante, to create something of our own.

We believe beautiful hair should complement who you are. We are drawn to kinky straight because it is a beautiful middle ground: the fullness and character of natural hair, with the versatility of straight styling.
