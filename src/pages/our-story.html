Zama + Asante

Born from the bond between a mother and daughter, Zante Beauty began in 2021 and launched nationwide in 2026. Our name brings together Zama and Asante to create something uniquely ours.

Inspired by the beauty of kinky straight hair, we celebrate healthy hair journeys, styling freedom and the confidence to wear your hair your way.
