3. Our products

  • Our units are 100% virgin human hair. Because it is natural hair, small differences in colour, texture and shine between units are normal and are not defects.
  • Our signature kinky straight texture is not bone straight. After washing, moisture or humidity, silk-pressed or heat-styled hair returns to its natural blow-out texture.
  • Lengths are measured as true length. Density is 180% unless we state otherwise.
  • Photos and videos on this site are of real Zante units. Colours can look slightly different on different screens.
  • Products marked "Coming soon" are not on sale yet. Their prices are a guide and may change at launch.

5. Orders and Zante seasonal drops

  • Most units are made to order in scheduled production batches, called drops.
  • Orders close at 18:00 SAST on the first Monday and the third Monday of each month. An order placed after a cut-off joins the next drop.
  • After a drop closes, allow about 3 to 4 weeks for production, international shipping, inspection and pre-washing before local dispatch. This is an estimate, not a guaranteed date.
  • We send you an update by WhatsApp or email at each main step of your order.
  • If your unit is not dispatched within 6 weeks after your drop closes, you may cancel your order for a full refund, or choose to keep waiting (CPA section 19).

6. Cancelling an order

  • Before your drop closes: contact us and we will cancel your order and refund you in full.
  • After your drop closes but before dispatch: you may still cancel. However, because we have already placed your order with our supplier, we will charge a reasonable cancellation fee.
  • Because these are customised orders, the fee is 20% of your order plus the shipping cost. We will tell you this total before you decide (CPA section 17).
  • Contact us directly to ask about the final amount, as it differs from customer to customer.
  • The cooling-off period applies (see clause 8).

8. Cooling-off period of 7 days

  • Hair units are personal hygiene products. For a cooling-off return, the unit must be UNWORN, UNALTERED (no use of products) and UNSTYLED (in any way or form), in its original packaging, with all bands, attachments and TAMPER-EVIDENT HYGIENE SEALS INTACT. This is the hair tag on the unit, which must NOT be cut off. U-part openings must NOT be cut. This is because the unit will need to be resold, and you would not want to buy a unit that someone else has worn, altered or styled.
  • You pay the direct cost of sending the hair unit back. We will inspect the unit, and if it passes the hygiene inspection above (unworn, unaltered, unstyled, tamper-evident seals intact), we will process your refund within 30 days of receiving the returned product.

9. How to return a product

  1. Contact us via email with your order number (found on the hair tag), the reason for the return and clear photos of the unit.
  2. Wait for our return instructions before you send anything. We aim to reply to your email within 48 hours (excluding Sundays).
  3. Pack the product securely, in its original packaging.
  4. When it arrives, we inspect the product against your email and promptly let you know the outcome.
