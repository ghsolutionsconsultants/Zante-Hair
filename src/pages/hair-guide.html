Understanding our texture

Our signature kinky straight texture gives the fullness and subtle texture of natural-looking straight hair. It is not bone straight. Expect a soft, textured finish you can wear as a blow-out, sleek or curled.

Understanding density

All our units are 180% density. Density is the fullness of the hair, not the length of the unit.

True to length

Our hair is true length, so you get the full inches you pay for.

Why kinky straight?

Because your natural texture deserves a good match.

Not every hairstyle needs to start with a silk press, hours of installation or a full change to your natural hair. Kinky straight gives a beautiful balance between natural-looking texture and straight-hair versatility.

  • A more natural blend. Its textured finish complements naturally textured hair, so blending is easier when your leave-out is well matched.
  • Versatility. Wear it straight, add curls, create volume or try different everyday looks.
  • Less installation. Half wigs and U-part wigs are alternatives to sew-ins and more involved installs.
  • Styling freedom. Switch looks without committing to a long-term install.
  • Gentle on your journey. Enjoy added length and fullness while you keep up with your own hair growth.

Silk press guide

Want it sleek? Silk press at home.

Every Zante unit arrives in its natural kinky straight texture. We no longer offer silk pressing as a service, but here is a guide on how to achieve a silk press on your unit:

  • Always make sure your unit is freshly washed and free of products.
  • Spray clean water and brush it into the hair, then apply a good heat protector of your choice.
  • Optional, but effective: straighten your unit's kinks with a blow dryer and comb.
  • Apply heat protection again and set your hair straightener to a moderate to high temperature. Please use your discretion.
  • Straighten your unit in small sections, from root to tip (1 to 2 wefts at a time).
  • Finish off with a good anti-humidity product.

Disclaimer: the texture returns after washing, moisture or humidity. However, the more you silk press the unit, the fewer kinks it will have over time, which will affect how your unit's blowout finish looks.
