HThe Half Wig

A wig with minimal or no leave-out. Your own hair at the front is left out and blended in with the wig. This wig can be enjoyed in a silk pressed or blowout state. Style as desired.

  • No parting, no glue, no lace
  • On in minutes, grab-and-go
  • Wear it with a headband, or as a ponytail or a flip-over hairstyle
  • Removable and reusable

How it differs from the U-Part: no parting and no leave-out on top. It is quicker to put on, and your hairline does the blending.
