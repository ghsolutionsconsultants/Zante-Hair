3. Our products

  • Our units are 100% virgin human hair. Because it is natural hair, small differences in colour, texture and shine between units are normal and are not defects.
  • Our signature kinky straight texture is not bone straight. After washing, moisture or humidity, silk-pressed or heat-styled hair returns to its natural blow-out texture.
  • Lengths are measured as true length. Density is 180% unless we state otherwise.
  • Photos and videos on this site are of real Zante units. Colours can look slightly different on different screens.
  • Products marked "Coming soon" are not on sale yet. Their prices are a guide and may change at launch.

4. Prices and payment

  • All prices are in South African Rand (R). The price you pay is the price shown at checkout, plus the delivery fee shown before you pay.
  • You pay the full price when you order: by card on Yoco’s secure payment page, or as we agree with you on WhatsApp.
  • Card payments are made on Yoco’s secure payment page. We do not see or store your card details.
  • If a price on our site is clearly wrong because of an obvious error, we will contact you before we process your order. You can then pay the correct price or cancel for a full refund (CPA section 23).

5. Orders and Zante seasonal drops

  • Most units are made to order in scheduled production batches, called drops.
  • Orders close at 18:00 SAST on the first Monday and the third Monday of each month. An order placed after a cut-off joins the next drop.
  • After a drop closes, allow about 3 to 4 weeks for production, international shipping, inspection and pre-washing before local dispatch. This is an estimate, not a guaranteed date.
  • We send you an update by WhatsApp or email at each main step of your order.
  • If your unit is not dispatched within 6 weeks after your drop closes, you may cancel your order for a full refund, or choose to keep waiting (CPA section 19).

6. Cancelling an order

  • Before your drop closes: contact us and we will cancel your order and refund you in full.
  • After your drop closes but before dispatch: you may still cancel. Because we have already placed your order with our supplier, we may charge a reasonable cancellation fee. The fee will never be more than the costs we actually cannot recover, and we will tell you the amount before you decide (CPA section 17).
  • After you receive your order, see the cooling-off period in clause 8.

7. Delivery

  • We dispatch orders on Mondays. You choose how your order travels at checkout: Pick n Pay store to door (R90, 24 to 72 hours), PAXI store to store (R60 for 7 to 9 days, or R110 for 3 to 5 days), PostNet to PostNet (R109) or Aramex to your door (quoted). The fee and estimated time are shown at checkout.
  • Courier times are estimates and start after dispatch. Public holidays, remote areas and courier delays can add time.
  • You must give us a correct delivery address and contact number. If a parcel cannot be delivered because the details were wrong, or a PAXI or PostNet parcel is not collected in time, we may ask you to pay the extra courier cost to send it again.
  • Please check your parcel when you receive it (CPA section 19). If it is damaged, incomplete, or the wrong product was sent, please tell us as soon as possible, ideally within 7 days, and we will put it right at no cost to you.
  • The product becomes your responsibility once it is delivered to you or collected by you.

8. 7-day cooling-off period

Because you buy online, you may cancel your purchase without a reason and without a penalty within 7 days after you receive the goods (ECTA section 44).

  • Hair units are personal hygiene products. For a cooling-off return, the unit must be unworn, unaltered and unstyled, in its original packaging, with all bands, attachments and tamper-evident hygiene seals intact. U-part openings must not be cut.
  • You pay the direct cost of sending the product back. We do not charge any other fee.
  • We refund the full price you paid, including the original delivery fee, within 30 days of receiving the returned product.
  • This does not affect your rights if a product is defective (clause 9).

9. Defective products

Every product we sell must be of good quality, work as described and be free of defects (CPA sections 55 and 56).

  • If a product is defective, unsafe or not as described, you may return it within 6 months of delivery. You choose whether we repair it, replace it or refund you.
  • For defects, we pay the reasonable cost of the return.
  • Examples of defects include excessive shedding beyond normal, a faulty weft, clip, comb or band, or a unit that is clearly not the length, texture or density you ordered.
  • The following are not defects: normal wear and tear, and damage caused by chemical processing, colouring or bleaching, excessive heat, cutting or altering the unit, or not following our care guidance. Natural variation between human hair units (clause 3) is also not a defect.

10. How to return a product

  1. Contact us with your order number and the reason for the return. Photos help for a defect.
  2. Wait for our return instructions before you send anything.
  3. Pack the product securely, in its original packaging where possible.
  4. We inspect the product when it arrives and let you know the outcome.

11. Refunds

  • We refund to the payment method you used, within 30 days after we receive and inspect the returned product. We aim to do it within 10 business days.
