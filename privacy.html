Privacy policy

How Zante Beauty collects, uses and protects your personal information, in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA). We collect only what we need, we never sell it, and you stay in control of it.

In short

  • We only collect what we need to make, deliver and support your order.
  • We never sell or rent your personal information.
  • Reviews are shown without your name.
  • Marketing only with your permission. You can unsubscribe at any time.
  • You can ask us to show, correct or delete your information.

1. Who is responsible for your information

Zante Beauty is the "responsible party" under POPIA. The owner of the business is our Information Officer.

2. What information we collect

When you order

  • Your name, phone number, email address and delivery address or PostNet branch.
  • What you ordered: product, length, quantity, price and your drop.
  • Payment status and the payment reference. We never see or store your full card details. Your payment provider handles them.

When you contact us

  • The messages you send us by WhatsApp, email or social media, and our replies.

When you write a review

  • Your rating, the product and length, your review text, and optionally your order number and city.
  • We do not show your name. Your order number is used only to confirm that you are a Zante client and is never published.

When you join our drop reminders

  • Your email address.

When you visit this website

  • Our website host and font provider automatically receive technical information, such as your IP address and browser type, so they can show you the page.
  • We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. See clause 9.

3. Why we use your information

POPIA allows us to use your information only for a clear, lawful purpose. We use it to:

  • Process and deliver your order, including placing your order in a production drop and sending it to you. This is necessary to carry out our agreement with you.
  • Send you order updates by WhatsApp or email at each milestone, so you always know where your hair is.
  • Handle returns, refunds and warranty claims under the Consumer Protection Act.
  • Answer your questions and complaints.
  • Publish your review, without your name, when you have agreed to this.
  • Send drop reminders and offers, only if you have agreed (clause 4).
  • Keep financial records as the law requires, for example for tax.

We do not use your information for any other purpose without telling you first, and we do not make automated decisions about you.

4. Drop reminders and marketing

  • We send drop reminders, new arrivals and offers only if you have opted in, or if you are an existing customer and we tell you how to opt out (POPIA section 69).
  • Every marketing message tells you how to unsubscribe. You can also reply "STOP" on WhatsApp or ask us at any time.
  • Order updates are not marketing. You receive them for as long as you have an active order.

5. Who we share your information with

We never sell, rent or trade your personal information. We share only what is needed with service providers who help us run the business. They may use it only for that service and must keep it safe (POPIA sections 20 and 21):

  • Payment providers, such as PayFast and PayJustNow, to process your payment.
  • Couriers, such as PostNet and Aramex, to deliver your parcel.
  • Our hair supplier, which receives only order details such as product, length and quantity, never your name or contact details.
  • Technology providers that host our website, database, email and messaging, such as GitHub, Supabase, Google and WhatsApp.
  • Authorities, only where the law requires it.

6. Information stored outside South Africa

Some of our technology providers store information on servers outside South Africa. We use only providers that protect personal information to a standard similar to POPIA, through their own laws or binding agreements (POPIA section 72).

7. How long we keep your information

  • Order and payment records: 5 years after your order, because tax law requires it.
  • Messages with us: up to 2 years after your last order or message.
  • Reviews: for as long as they are on our site, or until you ask us to remove them.
  • Drop reminder email: until you unsubscribe.

After that, we delete or anonymise your information (POPIA section 14).

8. How we protect your information

  • Our website uses a secure connection (HTTPS).
  • Our order database can only be opened by Zante Beauty with a password. The public website can only add new reviews and read approved ones.
  • We limit who can see your information and keep it only as long as we need it.
  • If we ever believe your information has been accessed without permission, we will tell you and the Information Regulator as soon as reasonably possible (POPIA section 22).

9. Your device and this website

  • This website saves a few small settings on your own device only: your bag, your light or dark mode choice, and any review you have submitted until it reaches us. They are not sent to us and you can clear them in your browser settings.
  • We do not use advertising, tracking or analytics cookies. If we add them in future, we will update this policy and ask for your permission where the law requires it.
  • Our fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address to deliver them.

10. Your rights

Under POPIA, you have the right to:

  • Know whether we hold information about you, and get a copy of it (section 23).
  • Correct or delete information that is wrong, out of date or no longer needed (section 24).
  • Object to us using your information, for example for marketing (section 11).
  • Withdraw your consent at any time. This does not affect what we did before you withdrew it.
  • Complain to the Information Regulator (clause 12).

To use any of these rights, contact us using the details in clause 1. We may ask you to confirm who you are first, to protect your information. We reply within 30 days and do not charge for simple requests.

11. Children

Our products and website are for adults. We do not knowingly collect information about anyone under 18 without the consent of a parent or guardian. If you believe a child has given us their information, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Complaints

  1. Contact us first, and we will try to solve it quickly.
  2. If you are not happy with our answer, you can complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa) at inforegulator.org.za.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our business or the law changes. The date at the top shows the latest version. If we make an important change, we will tell customers with an active order.
