HThe Half Wig

A wig that covers the back of your head. Your own hair at the front is left out and blended in, usually finished with a headband.

  • No parting, no glue, no lace
  • On in minutes, grab-and-go
  • Wear it with a headband, ponytail or half up
  • Removable and reusable

How it differs from the U-Part: no parting and no leave-out on top. It is quicker to put on, and your hairline does the blending.

Half Wig or U-Part?

UU-Part Wig: a full wig with a U-shaped opening. A little of your own hair makes a natural parting.
