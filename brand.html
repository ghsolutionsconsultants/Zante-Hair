The new Zante mark

Same soul. Sharper at every size.

The new logo keeps what people already know about Zante: the mauve, the glossy bubbles and the flowing silk. It turns them into a mark that works on a 16-pixel browser tab, a satin bag, a bundle band and a billboard.

Before and after

What changed, and why

  • KeptZante Mauve as the hero colour, soft rounded strokes from the bubble lettering, and the misty silk mood.
  • ChangedThe background artwork is no longer part of the logo. It becomes a brand texture you can use behind the logo instead.
  • AddedA monogram for social avatars, favicons and packaging, plus a pearl that ties every touchpoint together.

Anatomy

Three strands. One pearl.

The Z monogram

Three strands of hair, in aubergine, mauve and silk mist, run together to form a Z. The diagonal is a soft body wave instead of a straight line, so the letter reads as hair. The strands start at a pearl, taken from the bubbles in the original artwork.

The wordmark

Custom monoline capitals with round ends, a grown-up version of the bubble letters. The A carries the signature: a pearl crown at the top and a body-wave crossbar. Wide letter spacing keeps it calm and premium.

Rules and files

Keep it clean

Do

  • Leave clear space around the logo equal to the height of the pearl times three.
  • Use the monogram alone below 120 pixels wide. Use the full lockup above that.
  • Place the logo on Pearl, Aubergine Ink or Zante Mauve.
  • Use the misty silk artwork as a background behind the logo, not inside it.
