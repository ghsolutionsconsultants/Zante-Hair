Make it last

The five-step care ritual

Keep scrolling. The strand follows you.

01

Detangle

Start at the ends with a wide-tooth comb, then work up toward the weft. Never comb from the root down.

Tool: wide-tooth comb

02

Wash

Cool water and a sulfate-free shampoo. Smooth downward strokes only. Never rub or twist.

Every 8 to 10 wears

03

Condition

Mid-lengths to ends for 10 minutes. Keep conditioner off the lace so the knots stay tight.

10 minutes, ends only

04

Air-dry

Pat with a microfibre towel, then dry on a wig stand. If you use heat, stay under 180 °C.

Max 180 °C

05

Store

Loose braid, satin bag, cool and dry place. It will be ready for the next install.

Satin, never plastic
