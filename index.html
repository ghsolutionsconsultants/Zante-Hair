HWhat is a Half Wig?

A wig that covers the back of your head. Your own hair at the front is left out and blended in, usually finished with a headband.

  • No parting, no glue, no lace
  • On in about 5 minutes
  • Removable and reusable

UWhat is a U-Part Wig?

A full wig with a U-shaped opening on top. You leave out a small section of your own hair to cover it, for a natural middle or side parting.

  • Natural parting, no lace frontal
  • Sew-in look, but removable
  • Does not come silk pressed
