Make it last

The five-step care ritual

Your kit: wide-tooth comb, sulfate-free shampoo, satin bag

01

Detangle

Start at the ends with a wide-tooth comb, then work up to the weft.

02

Wash

Cool water, sulfate-free shampoo, downward strokes only. Never rub.

03

Condition

Mid-lengths to ends for 10 minutes. Keep it off the lace knots.

04

Air-dry

Pat dry, then dry on a wig stand. If you use heat, stay under 180 °C.

05

Store

Loose braid, satin bag, cool and dry. Ready for the next install.
